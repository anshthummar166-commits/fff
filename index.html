Damage Profile

Small damage.
Big difference.

Accidents happen during renovations, tenancy transitions, and everyday life. We rectify all damage types on-site.

Chips →

Edge chipping in kitchen worktops, basins, vanity units, and tiled corners.

Scratches →

Fine abrasions and gouges across hardwood floors, glass, stainless steel, and resin.

Dents →

Impact indentations on architectural doors, composite cabinets, and veneer panels.

Burns →

Heat scorches and cigarette or iron burns on laminates, timber, and Corian surfaces.

Holes →

Drill mis-locations, fixings holes, cable penetration points, and hollow core punctures.

Cracks →

Structural stress fractures in ceramic baths, shower trays, basins, and natural tiles.

Scuffs →

Surface friction markings on powder-coated metals, UPVC frames, and architectural trims.

Colour Damage →

Sun bleaching, chemical staining, and discolouration seamlessly blended to original tone.

Our Methodology

How it works.

A streamlined four-step workflow delivering invisible on-site repairs with zero hassle.

01

Send Photos

Send us clear photographs of the damaged area along with your postcode. We evaluate lighting, texture, and substrate complexity.

02

Assessment & Quote

We identify the exact surface compound, determine the restoration protocol, and provide a fixed transparent quote with no upfront payment.

03

On-Site Repair

Our master technician arrives with bespoke pigments and fillers, rebuilding the surface contour and mimicking natural grain and texture.

04

Back to Beautiful

The repaired area cures and seamlessly blends into the surrounding surface. Finished to the exact original gloss or satin sheen.
